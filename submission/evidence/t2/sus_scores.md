# Task 2 — SUS scores (Scenario D)

**Formula (standard SUS):** for each participant, contribution =  
- odd items (1,3,5,7,9): `score − 1`  
- even items (2,4,6,8,10): `5 − score`  
then **SUS = (sum of 10 contributions) × 2.5** → scale 0–100.

Benchmark: industry average ≈ **68** (Sauro / Bangor adjective mapping often treats ~68 as average).

| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| Mean SUS | **55.0** |

| Rough adjective band (Bangor et al.) | ~55 ≈ **OK** / low-OK — usable with friction, not “Good” (~73+) |
| Spread | 35.0 (P5) … 75.0 (P1) — guests scored lower; students higher but still mixed |

Interpretation for the report: the support-request lifecycle is completable for experienced students, but guests hit localisation, validation placement, and re-find friction that pull the mean below the common 68 benchmark.
